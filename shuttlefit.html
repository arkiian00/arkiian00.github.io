Overview

ShuttleFit is an AI-powered badminton posture assessment app developed for a Korean startup competition. The project aimed to introduce the fun, energetic, and passionate culture of badminton to a wider audience while establishing a visual identity that intuitively communicates the service’s core value: helping players identify and improve their posture through AI-driven feedback.

Strategy

The brand’s visual concept was inspired by the moment a shuttlecock strikes the ground with force after a powerful badminton smash. This dynamic moment became the central motif, capturing two core values: the explosive energy of the sport and the precision essential to posture assessment.

The strategy was to create a visual identity that evokes the excitement and intensity of badminton while conveying a sense of accuracy and reliability through a sharp, focused visual direction.

Execution

The primary symbol was designed to capture the dynamic moment a shuttlecock hits the ground. Its rounded cork tip represents a precise landing, reflecting the service’s ability to identify correct posture. The sharp feathers radiating outward symbolise both the accuracy of AI-driven assessment and the explosive energy of badminton players.

To ensure consistency across digital and print applications, I established minimum size guidelines and defined clear colour usage rules, including restrictions on using secondary colours as backgrounds to avoid visual conflicts with the primary brand colour. Together, these guidelines formed a cohesive and systematic logo identity system.
