Overview

Wellow allows users to instantly search and translate words on their mobile devices without leaving their current screen or app. By providing a floating dock that overlays any app like Safari or Kindle, it helps maintain a seamless reading flow without interruptions.

Strategy

This app addresses the inconvenience and loss of concentration caused by switching to dictionary apps when encountering unknown words. It utilizes the "Display Over Other Apps" permission to create an environment where users can check meanings instantly, fully preserving their context and focus.

03 SETTINGS & EXIT

Design

The design uses warm beige and off-white backgrounds to reduce eye strain, paired with calming navy and blue accents to enhance readability. It combines professional serif fonts with clean sans-serif fonts for clear information delivery. The minimalist floating dock can be freely dragged anywhere to avoid obstructing the screen

Execution

Onboarding clearly guides users to grant overlay permissions, set translation languages, and learn usage through a dock-dragging tutorial. While reading, selecting a word and tapping the dock displays an instant translation popup. Expanding it reveals detailed meanings and synonyms in a clean card UI, supporting deeper learning.
