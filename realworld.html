Overview

REALWORLD is an interactive storytelling platform that transforms stories into real-world experiences. Collaborating with the popular Korean animation Catch! Teenieping, this project introduced an interactive treasure hunt game for young children. I was responsible for developing the key visual and promotional graphics, translating the animation\'s playful identity into a cohesive visual experience across digital and physical touchpoints.

Catch! Teenieping: My Heart's Rainbow

Inspired by the animation's playful and magical atmosphere, I developed a fairytale-inspired key visual using pastel colours, soft clouds, and a fantasy castle. To preserve the original character IP, I created AI-generated 3D-style backgrounds and refined them in Photoshop before integrating the approved character assets, maintaining visual consistency while introducing a new environment for the interactive game.

National Marketing Design

To promote the interactive experience and the Teenieping flagship store, I designed marketing materials across digital and offline environments. The designs included in-store digital signage and promotional graphics, combining playful typography, character illustrations, and clear information layouts to communicate the campaign effectively while maintaining a consistent visual identity.
