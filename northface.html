Overview

This personal rebranding project explores how The North Face White Label could establish a more distinctive identity within the lifestyle market. Inspired by The North Face Purple Label in Japan, I reinterpreted the brand's outdoor heritage through an urban perspective, developing a visual identity that connects everyday city life with the spirit of exploration.

Period

2024

Strategy

To address White Label's lack of differentiation, I established three core values—Lifestyle, Exploration, and Heritage—and translated them into the design keywords Urban, Crosswalk, and Vintage. Inspired by the brand's philosophy, "Never Stop Exploring," I used the pedestrian crossing as a central metaphor for discovering new experiences within familiar urban environments, forming the foundation of the rebranding concept.

Execution

The rebranding concept was translated into a cohesive visual identity across multiple touchpoints, including logo design, campaign graphics, spatial design, and fashion applications. Inspired by pedestrian crossings, the graphic system combines bold typography, hand-drawn elements, and a restrained colour palette to express an urban, vintage aesthetic. This visual language was extended into digital campaigns, a pop-up store concept, and apparel graphics, creating a consistent brand experience that connects everyday urban life with the spirit of exploration.
